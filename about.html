Levi Spectre is Associate Professor of Philosophy at the Open University of Israel, where he heads the PPE program (Philosophy, Economics and Political Science), and an affiliated researcher in the Department of Philosophy at Stockholm University. He works in epistemology, traditional and formal, and on theoretical and pragmatic rationality. He received his PhD from Stockholm University in 2009.
